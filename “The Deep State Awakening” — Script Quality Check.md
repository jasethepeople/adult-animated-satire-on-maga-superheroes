## Storyboard Priorities

The first storyboard pass should preserve the following comic beats: the hair wave recognizing danger; the Commander-in-Chief losing to a hallway; the Questioner defeating a scanner with a definition request; Red Tape becoming entangled in his own process; the Info Warrior’s blank-folder reveal; the Enforcer’s one-sentence plan; and the final city-wide “make a decision” beat.

The pilot should keep action quick, clear, and non-graphic. Pause for reactions after the dry jokes, particularly where the Spectral Mentor treats a minor logistical issue as a national moment of destiny.
