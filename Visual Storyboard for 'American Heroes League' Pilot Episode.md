# Visual Storyboard for 'American Heroes League' Pilot Episode

## Animation Style Guide

### Overall Style
- **Animation Type**: 2D traditional animation with digital enhancements
- **Visual Reference**: Similar to FreedomToons but with more detailed backgrounds and fluid movement
- **Color Palette**: Bright, saturated colors with strong contrast between heroes (vibrant, patriotic) and villains (drab, bureaucratic)

### Character Design Principles
- **Heroes**: Exaggerated physical features that emphasize their personalities and powers
- **Villains**: Abstract, conceptual designs that embody their bureaucratic/authoritarian nature
- **Proportions**: Cartoonishly distorted with emphasis on recognizable features (Trump's hair, Elon's elongated body, etc.)
- **Expressions**: Highly exaggerated facial expressions that change dramatically for comedic effect

### Animation Techniques
- **Movement**: Snappy, energetic movement for heroes; rigid, mechanical movement for villains
- **Special Effects**: Bright, comic-book style effects for superpowers (speed lines, impact stars, etc.)
- **Scene Transitions**: Dynamic wipes and transitions with patriotic motifs
- **Background Style**: Simplified but recognizable real-world locations with satirical elements

## Animation Notes

### Character Movement Specifics
- **Trump**: Hair moves independently from body, hands grow and shrink dramatically
- **Elon**: Body stretches and contorts like rubber, hair morphs between styles
- **Tucker**: Head tilts at impossible angles, eyebrows arch beyond his hairline
- **Alex Jones**: Face cycles through color spectrum from pink to deep red when excited
- **JFK's Ghost**: Floats with ethereal trail, occasionally phases through solid objects

### Scene Transitions
- Use star-wipe transitions with red, white, and blue stars
- For location changes, employ a flying eagle transition
- For time jumps, show a clock with hands spinning rapidly

### Sound Design Notes
- Patriotic music swells during hero moments
- Bureaucratic, monotonous tones for villain scenes
- Exaggerated sound effects for power usage (giant hand growth, confusion ray, etc.)
- Each character should have signature sound effects that accompany their movements
