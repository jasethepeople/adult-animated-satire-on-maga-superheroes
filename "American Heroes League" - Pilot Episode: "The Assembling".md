# "American Heroes League" - Pilot Episode: "The Assembling"

FADE IN:

## ACT ONE

### SCENE 1 - MAR-A-LAGO ESTATE - DAY

*Establishing shot of Mar-a-Lago, palm trees swaying in the breeze. The property looks like a cross between a luxury resort and a superhero headquarters with subtle American flag motifs.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
America... once the land of the free, home of the brave. But lately, something's not right.

*DONALD TRUMP sits on his golden patio chair, squinting at the horizon. His hair is comically large and swooshes independently in the opposite direction of the wind. His hands appear normal-sized until he reaches for his iced tea, at which point they grow dramatically.*

**TRUMP**
(to himself)
Something's wrong with America. I can feel it. It's not tremendous anymore. Not tremendous at all.

*Trump's phone BUZZES. The screen shows "ECONOMY ALERT: Small Businesses Closing at Record Rate."*

**TRUMP**
(frowning)
Sad. Very sad.

*Trump stands up, his posture becoming heroic as patriotic music begins to play softly.*

**TRUMP**
I may not be in the White House anymore, but I still have a duty. A tremendous duty.

*His suit TRANSFORMS into a superhero outfit with red, white, and blue colors and a "CIC" emblem (Commander-in-Chief).*

**TRUMP**
The people need me. They need... The Commander-in-Chief!

### SCENE 2 - WASHINGTON D.C. - DEPARTMENT OF BUREAUCRACY - DAY

*A massive, gray building with endless floors. The sign keeps extending upward beyond the frame.*

*Inside, THE BUREAUCRAT (a villain made entirely of scrolling government forms with multiple arms stamping "DENIED" on everything) stands before a massive machine labeled "REGULATION GENERATOR 5000."*

**THE BUREAUCRAT**
(laughing mechanically)
With each new regulation, their freedom diminishes! Soon, the American spirit will be crushed under the weight of paperwork!

*The machine PULSES, sending out waves of red tape that flow out the windows toward small businesses in the distance. Each pulse causes a small business sign to flip from "OPEN" to "CLOSED."*

**THE BUREAUCRAT**
The Great Reset begins with the small businesses. Then, we take on the entire economy!

### SCENE 3 - SKIES ABOVE WASHINGTON D.C. - DAY

*Trump flies through the air, his tie flapping behind him like a cape, his hair remaining perfectly still despite the wind.*

**TRUMP**
(into earpiece)
This is Commander-in-Chief. I'm detecting high levels of regulation in the D.C. area. Very high. The highest.

*Trump's eyes GLOW as he activates his "Fake News Detector" vision, scanning the city below. He zooms in on the Department of Bureaucracy.*

**TRUMP**
Bingo. The best bingo. Everyone says so.

### SCENE 4 - DEPARTMENT OF BUREAUCRACY - MOMENTS LATER

*Trump CRASHES through the ceiling, striking a heroic pose as he lands.*

**TRUMP**
Your regulatory reign of terror ends now!

*The Bureaucrat turns, surprised.*

**THE BUREAUCRAT**
Former President Trump! Or should I say... Commander-in-Chief? You're too late! My regulations are already strangling the economy!

*Trump's hands GROW to massive size as he lunges toward the machine.*

**TRUMP**
I'll just have to use my executive power to overturn these regulations!

*The Bureaucrat shoots red tape from his multiple arms, wrapping Trump up.*

**THE BUREAUCRAT**
You can't fight bureaucracy alone! For every regulation you cut, I create two more!

*Trump struggles against the red tape but is becoming increasingly entangled.*

**TRUMP**
(straining)
Need... to make... America... great... again...

*The Bureaucrat approaches the bound Trump, looming over him.*

**THE BUREAUCRAT**
The era of American exceptionalism is over. Soon, The Great Reset will ensure total government control!

*Trump's eyes widen.*

**TRUMP**
The Great Reset? I knew it! Alex Jones was right!

*The Bureaucrat laughs as the Regulation Generator pulses more intensely.*

### SCENE 5 - MAR-A-LAGO - NIGHT

*Trump sits in his office, bandaged and defeated. News reports show small businesses closing across the country.*

**TRUMP**
(to himself)
I can't beat them alone. The Deep State is too powerful. Too organized.

*Suddenly, the room TEMPERATURE DROPS. A spectral blue light fills the room.*

**TRUMP**
What in the name of covfefe?

*The ghostly form of JFK appears, glowing with red, white, and blue ethereal energy.*

**JFK'S GHOST**
(with classic Kennedy accent)
The torch has been passed to a new generation, Donald.

**TRUMP**
(shocked)
JFK? But you're... you know... not alive anymore. Believe me, I know dead. I've seen dead. The best dead.

**JFK'S GHOST**
I am the Eternal Patriot now. I appear when America needs guidance.

*JFK's ghost waves his hand, and a holographic display appears showing various potential heroes.*

**JFK'S GHOST**
You cannot defeat the Deep State alone. You need a team. A league of American heroes.

*Images appear of ELON MUSK, TUCKER CARLSON, DAN BONGINO, ALEX JONES, OWEN SHROYER, and TULSI GABBARD.*

**TRUMP**
These people? But they're not superheroes. Except maybe Elon. He's pretty super. Built some tremendous rockets.

**JFK'S GHOST**
They all have unique abilities that, when combined, can counter the Deep State's plans. The Great Reset can be stopped, but only if you unite.

*JFK's ghost begins to fade.*

**TRUMP**
Wait! How do I find them? How do I convince them?

**JFK'S GHOST**
(fading)
Ask not what your country can do for you...

*Trump finishes the quote as JFK disappears.*

**TRUMP**
...ask what you can do for your country.

*Trump stands, determination on his face.*

**TRUMP**
Time to make a tremendous team. The best team. Everyone will say so.

## ACT TWO

### SCENE 6 - SPACEX LAUNCH FACILITY - DAY

*A massive rocket stands ready for launch. ELON MUSK oversees final preparations, his body unnaturally elongated and his hair morphing between different styles every few seconds.*

*Trump approaches, now in his civilian clothes but with his "Commander-in-Chief" outfit visible underneath.*

**TRUMP**
Elon! Just the genius I wanted to see.

**ELON**
(surprised)
Mr. President? What brings you to my launch facility? Are you finally taking me up on that Mars colony offer?

**TRUMP**
I need your help. America needs your help. The Deep State is implementing something called "The Great Reset."

*Elon's eyes briefly flash with Twitter/X logos.*

**ELON**
I've been monitoring unusual activity in the financial markets and social media censorship patterns. Something big is happening.

**TRUMP**
I tried to stop one of them - The Bureaucrat - but he was too powerful.

**ELON**
(nodding)
Bureaucracy is the natural enemy of innovation. That's why I keep moving my companies to different states.

*Suddenly, ALARMS BLARE. A screen shows satellites being disabled around Earth.*

**TECHNICIAN (O.S.)**
Mr. Musk! Starlink satellites are going offline! Someone's hacking the system!

*Elon's posture changes, becoming more heroic.*

**ELON**
Looks like we have a demonstration of the problem.

*Elon's business attire transforms into a futuristic superhero suit with an "X" emblem.*

**ELON**
I've been developing some technology on the side. Call it my "Techno King" project.

*Trump grins and rips open his shirt to reveal his Commander-in-Chief costume.*

**TRUMP**
That's tremendous. Really tremendous.

### SCENE 7 - SPACEX CONTROL ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

*Elon's fingers move at impossible speeds across multiple keyboards. Trump watches, impressed.*

**ELON**
The hack is coming from the Department of Digital Control. It's The Silencer - the Deep State's censorship specialist.

*On screen, a villain made of social media ban notices with a permanently sealed mouth appears.*

**TRUMP**
I know that guy! He banned me from Twitter before you bought it!

**ELON**
He's trying to take down global communications. First step of The Great Reset - control the information.

*Elon activates his Neural Link power, his eyes glowing as he mentally connects to the systems.*

**ELON**
I can hold them off temporarily, but we need more help.

**TRUMP**
JFK's ghost gave me a list. Tucker Carlson is next.

**ELON**
(typing furiously)
I'll stabilize the satellites. You get Tucker. We'll need his Confusion Ray to counter The Silencer's propaganda.

*Trump nods and heads for the exit.*

**TRUMP**
Make technology great again, Elon!

**ELON**
(calling after him)
And Donald - this team needs a name!

**TRUMP**
(grinning)
I'm thinking... American Heroes League. Tremendous name. The best name.

### SCENE 8 - FOX NEWS STUDIO - EVENING

*TUCKER CARLSON sits at his desk, preparing for his show. His expression is permanently confused, and his eyebrows are arched impossibly high.*

*A PRODUCER approaches.*

**PRODUCER**
Two minutes to air, Tucker.

*Tucker nods, then suddenly TILTS HIS HEAD at an impossible angle, sensing something.*

**TUCKER**
Someone's hiding the truth. I can feel it.

*Trump and Elon burst into the studio.*

**TRUMP**
Tucker! America needs you!

**TUCKER**
(eyebrows arching even higher)
Former President Trump? Elon Musk? What exactly is going on here?

**ELON**
The Deep State is implementing The Great Reset. They're taking down communications and strangling businesses with regulations.

**TUCKER**
(to camera, though not on air)
Why is no one talking about this? What don't they want you to know?

*Tucker's bow tie begins to SPIN as he processes the information.*

**TRUMP**
We're forming a team. The American Heroes League. We need your questioning powers.

*Suddenly, all the studio screens FLASH with the image of THE SILENCER.*

**THE SILENCER**
(through text since his mouth is sealed)
THIS BROADCAST HAS BEEN DEEMED MISINFORMATION. PREPARE FOR CANCELLATION.

*The studio lights begin to flicker.*

**TUCKER**
(standing, determined)
I've been waiting for this moment my entire career.

*Tucker's casual attire transforms into a superhero outfit with a giant question mark emblem.*

**TUCKER**
Call me... The Questioner!

### SCENE 9 - FOX NEWS STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

*The Silencer's digital form emerges from the screens, shooting censorship bars that begin to erase parts of the studio.*

**THE SILENCER**
(text appearing above him)
YOUR DANGEROUS SPEECH ENDS NOW.

*Tucker steps forward, his confusion expression intensifying.*

**TUCKER**
But what exactly is dangerous about asking questions? Isn't curiosity the foundation of knowledge?

*The Silencer pauses, momentarily confused.*

**TUCKER**
And who exactly decides what constitutes "misinformation"? By what standard? According to whom?

*The Silencer clutches his head as Tucker's Confusion Ray takes effect.*

**ELON**
It's working! Keep questioning him!

*Trump steps forward, hands growing.*

**TRUMP**
And while he's confused...

*Trump CLAPS his giant hands together, creating a shockwave that disrupts The Silencer's digital form.*

**THE SILENCER**
(glitching)
SYSTEM... ERROR... NARRATIVE... DISRUPTED...

*The Silencer retreats back into the screens, which return to normal.*

**TUCKER**
(straightening his bow tie)
He'll be back. They always come back when you ask the real questions.

**TRUMP**
That's why we need to complete the team. JFK's ghost showed me who else we need.

**ELON**
(checking his phone)
My satellites detect unusual activity at the FBI headquarters. Something about "domestic terrorists."

**TUCKER**
(eyebrows raising)
Let me guess - they're labeling concerned parents as threats again?

**TRUMP**
That sounds like a job for Dan Bongino. Former Secret Service. Knows the Deep State from the inside.

**ELON**
And we should get Alex Jones. If anyone knows about the Great Reset conspiracy...

**TUCKER**
(nodding)
I'll contact Owen Shroyer too. We'll need his reporting skills to document everything.

**TRUMP**
And Tulsi Gabbard. She understands both sides of the political aisle.

*The three heroes stand together, determined.*

**TRUMP**
The American Heroes League is forming. And it's going to be tremendous. Believe me.

## ACT THREE

### SCENE 10 - FREEDOM HEADQUARTERS (CONVERTED TRUMP TOWER) - DAY

*A montage shows Trump, Elon, and Tucker recruiting the others:*

*- DAN BONGINO demonstrating his "Secret Service Sense" by detecting hidden Deep State agents*

*- ALEX JONES ripping his shirt off while ranting about globalists, his face turning increasingly red*

*- OWEN SHROYER using his "Bullhorn Blast" to expose corruption to a crowd*

*- TULSI GABBARD shifting between political alignments to confuse her opponents*

*The full team now gathers in a high-tech conference room with American flags and patriotic decorations.*

**TRUMP**
Thank you all for coming. Really appreciate it. The best people.

**ALEX JONES**
(intense, neck veins pulsing)
I'VE BEEN WARNING ABOUT THE GREAT RESET FOR YEARS! I HAVE THE DOCUMENTS!

*Alex pulls out a stack of papers from thin air.*

**TUCKER**
(head tilted)
What exactly do those documents say, Alex?

**ALEX**
(flipping through frantically)
THAT THE GLOBALISTS ARE USING REGULATIONS AND DIGITAL CONTROL TO IMPLEMENT WORLDWIDE TYRANNY!

**DAN BONGINO**
(shoulders broadening as he speaks)
As a former Secret Service agent, I can confirm there are elements within the government working against American interests.

**TULSI**
(the white streak in her hair glowing)
This isn't about left versus right. It's about freedom versus control.

**OWEN**
(microphone permanently attached to hand)
And the mainstream media is covering it all up! They're complicit!

**ELON**
(stretching his arm unnaturally to grab a tablet)
I've compiled data on their activities. The Bureaucrat's regulation wave has shut down 30% of small businesses. The Silencer has censored 50% of online speech.

*Trump stands at the head of the table, commanding attention.*

**TRUMP**
The Deep State thinks they can implement their Great Reset without resistance. But they didn't count on us. The American Heroes League.

*The temperature DROPS as JFK's ghost appears.*

**JFK'S GHOST**
The fate of the republic rests in your hands. The Deep State has gathered their forces.

*A holographic map appears showing the Department of Bureaucracy glowing red.*

**JFK'S GHOST**
They're centralizing their power. The Bureaucrat, The Silencer, The Globalist, Professor Indoctrination, and Spin Doctor - all gathered to launch the final phase.

**TRUMP**
Then that's where we strike. Together.

*The team puts their hands in the center of the table.*

**ALL**
AMERICAN HEROES LEAGUE!

### SCENE 11 - DEPARTMENT OF BUREAUCRACY - NIGHT

*The heroes approach the massive building, which is now surrounded by a swirling vortex of red tape, censorship bars, and propaganda headlines.*

**TRUMP**
Remember the plan. Elon and Tucker, take on The Silencer. Dan and Owen, handle Spin Doctor. Alex, you've got Professor Indoctrination. Tulsi, The Globalist is yours. I'll take The Bureaucrat.

*They nod in agreement.*

**ELON**
What if we fail?

*JFK's ghost appears briefly.*

**JFK'S GHOST**
We choose to do these things not because they are easy, but because they are hard.

*The ghost vanishes.*

**TRUMP**
(inspired)
We won't fail. We're going to win so much, you'll get tired of winning!

*The team charges forward, bursting through the entrance.*

### SCENE 12 - DEPARTMENT OF BUREAUCRACY - MAIN HALL - CONTINUOUS

*Inside, the five villains stand around a massive device labeled "GREAT RESET INITIATOR."*

**THE BUREAUCRAT**
(spotting the heroes)
Impossible! How did they find us?

**SPIN DOCTOR**
(head spinning 360 degrees)
Don't worry, I'll just change the narrative!

*Spin Doctor creates illusory headlines that float through the air: "HEROES ACTUALLY VILLAINS" and "GOVERNMENT SAVING ECONOMY."*

**TUCKER**
(eyebrows arching)
But what evidence supports those headlines? Where are your sources?

*Tucker's Confusion Ray hits Spin Doctor, causing his spinning head to stutter.*

*The battle erupts across the hall:*

*- Elon uses his Meme Magic to counter The Silencer's censorship beams*

*- Dan's Freedom Fist collides with Spin Doctor's propaganda shields*

*- Alex Jones, shirtless and red-faced, overwhelms Professor Indoctrination with rapid-fire conspiracy facts*

*- Tulsi shifts between political stances to confuse The Globalist*

*- Owen documents everything with his camera eyes*

*Trump confronts The Bureaucrat near the Great Reset device.*

**THE BUREAUCRAT**
(shooting red tape)
You cannot stop progress! The world will be remade in our image!

**TRUMP**
(dodging)
That's where you're wrong, kiddo.

*Trump's hands grow to enormous size as he begins tearing through the red tape.*

**TRUMP**
America doesn't need a reset. It needs to be great again!

*The Bureaucrat backs up against the device.*

**THE BUREAUCRAT**
You're too late! The Great Reset is already initiating!

*The device powers up, sending energy pulses throughout the building.*

**TRUMP**
(to team)
Heroes! Unite your powers!

*In a choreographed sequence:*

*- Tucker fires his Confusion Ray at the device*
*- Elon hacks into it with his Neural Link*
*- Dan punches it with his Freedom Fist*
*- Alex overloads it with conspiracy energy*
*- Owen broadcasts its weaknesses*
*- Tulsi strikes its political balance point*

*Trump delivers the final blow with his giant hands.*

**TRUMP**
You're fired!

*The device EXPLODES in a spectacular display of red, white, and blue energy.*

### SCENE 13 - WASHINGTON D.C. - DAWN

*The sun rises over D.C. as small businesses reopen, their "CLOSED" signs flipping back to "OPEN."*

*The American Heroes League stands triumphant on the steps of the Lincoln Memorial.*

**TRUMP**
We won this battle, but the war for America's soul continues.

**ELON**
The Deep State will regroup. They always do.

**TUCKER**
(to the others)
The question is: are we ready for what comes next?

*They look out at the awakening city.*

**TRUMP**
America is watching now. They're beginning to see the truth.

*JFK's ghost appears one final time, visible only to the heroes.*

**JFK'S GHOST**
The torch has been passed. Carry it well.

*The heroes strike a team pose as the camera pulls back to reveal American flags waving and citizens cheering.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
And so the American Heroes League was born. A team dedicated to fighting the Deep State, preserving freedom, and making America great again. Their adventures were just beginning...

*Post-credits scene: In a shadowy room, a mysterious figure watches news reports of the heroes' victory.*

**MYSTERIOUS FIGURE**
(face obscured)
Activate our sleeper agents. The Great Reset may have failed, but Project Dark Winter is ready to launch.

*The screen shows a weather map with unusual storm patterns forming over America.*

FADE TO BLACK.

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
American Heroes League will return in "Climate Control"...

THE END
