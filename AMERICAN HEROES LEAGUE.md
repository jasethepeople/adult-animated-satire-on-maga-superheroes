# AMERICAN HEROES LEAGUE
## “THE DEEP STATE AWAKENING”

**Format:** Adult animated satire pilot  
**Target runtime:** 20–22 minutes  
**Draft:** Production concept draft

> **FICTIONAL PARODY NOTICE:** This is an original adult animated satire in an alternate superhero universe. The cast comprises exaggerated fictional caricatures and abstract villain archetypes. It is not factual reporting, political advocacy, a real statement by anyone depicted, or endorsement by any person or organization. Final production should use original voice performances, original branding, and a visible parody notice.

---

## COLD OPEN

### INT. SUBLEVEL 900 — BUREAU OF POTENTIAL OUTCOMES — NIGHT

A vast underground conference chamber. At its center stands the **DEEP STATE AWAKENER**: a sleeping machine assembled from paper feeders, blank interface panels, chalkboard arms, a rotating teleprompter ring, and a giant red spiral button.

The machine snores like a filing cabinet being dragged across carpet.

**RED TAPE**, a folder-bodied bureaucracy monster with stamp hands and a ribbon tie, approaches the machine with **THE SILENCER**, a tall screen-bodied censor archetype; **SPIN DOCTOR**, a rotating-faced narrative manipulator; **ONE WORLD ORDER**, a globe-torso strategist in a map-panel coat; and **PROFESSOR INDOCTRINATION**, a floating brain in academic robes and tiny sneakers.

**SPIN DOCTOR**
Tonight, we wake the system. Then we explain that it was always awake.

**RED TAPE**
First, I require Form A-1: Intent to Begin Intending.

**ONE WORLD ORDER**
My preliminary framework contains only nine hundred pages.

He drops a binder. The floor buckles.

**PROFESSOR INDOCTRINATION**
Before activation, define “awake.” Is it biological? Institutional? A social agreement between a button and the people who fear it?

**THE SILENCER**
Your concern has been muted for efficiency.

The Professor mouths an offended silence.

Red Tape places a blank form over the spiral button. Spin Doctor reaches for a pen. One World Order unrolls a map across the floor. The Professor’s pointer extends. The Silencer’s cable arm accidentally plugs into the machine.

All five touch the button at once.

**THUNK.**

The Awakener’s office-light eyes open.

**DEEP STATE AWAKENER**
Please… wait.

Gray **ADMINISTRATIVE FOG** explodes from vents, carrying blank forms, loading wheels, and arrows that point to more arrows.

The villains grin. Then the fog produces five tiny forms in front of them.

**DEEP STATE AWAKENER** (CONT’D)
State purpose of awakening.

The villains stare at one another.

**SPIN DOCTOR**
We’ll circle back.

**SMASH CUT TO TITLE:**

# AMERICAN HEROES LEAGUE
## “THE DEEP STATE AWAKENING”

## ACT ONE

### EXT. CAPITALBURG — NIGHT

Fog sweeps through the fictional city. A bus freezes at a green light. A repair clerk holds a broken faucet and an invisible form.

**BUS DRIVER**
The light is green, but no one has approved forward.

**REPAIR CLERK**
I can fix this in thirty seconds. Or form a committee to study whether water is a construct.

A customer slowly retreats.

### INT. FREEDOM HEADQUARTERS — COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF’S OFFICE — NIGHT

**THE COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF** sits at an ornate desk. His oversized blond hair wave drifts opposite the ceiling fan. His black-gold **DEAL METER** flashes gray.

**DEAL METER**
Civic momentum: pending.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
Pending. Terrible word. Weak word. A word with no shoulders.

A delivery robot rolls in with a large blank form.

**DELIVERY ROBOT**
Emergency notice. Please provide emergency notice before accepting emergency notice.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
That’s not an emergency. That’s a bad meeting.

His navy suit brightens into a hero uniform. Red tie becomes a short cape. Gold `CIC` shield glows.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF** (CONT’D)
Time for a tremendous entrance.

He heads for the elevator.

**ELEVATOR VOICE**
Please select a floor.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
The crime floor.

**ELEVATOR VOICE**
There are many interpretations of crime.

The Commander-in-Chief turns.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
Balcony it is.

### INT. BUREAU OF POTENTIAL OUTCOMES — MAIN HALL — NIGHT

The Commander-in-Chief crashes through a skylight and waits for an echo. Nothing comes.

Red Tape emerges from a hallway of file cabinets.

**RED TAPE**
Unidentified dramatic entry. Please complete an impact assessment.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
I’m the Commander-in-Chief. You know who I am. Great profile. Tremendous silhouette.

**RED TAPE**
I cannot confirm a silhouette without Form S-11.

A form unfolds to the size of a parade banner.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
I’m not filling that out.

**RED TAPE**
Then you cannot decline it.

The Commander-in-Chief’s eyes flash blue.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
Fake News Detector.

A blue squint-ray scans the form. It reveals only anxious arrows leading to more forms.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF** (CONT’D)
I knew it. All arrows. Nothing good starts with arrows.

His hands grow. Gold handshake energy sparks.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF** (CONT’D)
Art of the Deal. You turn off the machine, I don’t redecorate this entire building in gold. Very fair.

Red Tape accepts the handshake with a stamp hand.

**RED TAPE**
I require a counteroffer review committee.

Red ribbons burst from the floor, wrapping the Commander-in-Chief’s wrists, tie-cape, and hair wave.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
I am very difficult to tie up.

**RED TAPE**
Appeals are available. Choose a door.

Three identical doors rise. Each bears a meaningless shape.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
What do they mean?

**RED TAPE**
That requires a briefing.

The Awakener groans below. Fog surges.

The Commander-in-Chief pulls free one hand. Two more forms wrap it.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
This hallway is dishonest.

Red Tape stamps the Commander-in-Chief’s chest; the gold `CIC` shield becomes a gray spiral.

**RED TAPE**
Your heroism is under review.

The floor opens. The Commander-in-Chief drops into a cubicle maze.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
(yelling)
I will leave a very strong review!

### INT. FREEDOM HEADQUARTERS — LATER

The Commander-in-Chief returns with forms draped over him. His hair wave droops.

Blue mist rises. **THE SPECTRAL MENTOR** materializes, immaculate charcoal suit glowing at the edges.

**SPECTRAL MENTOR**
Commander.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
You’re late.

**SPECTRAL MENTOR**
I have been dead for decades. My relationship with punctuality has evolved.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
Strong entrance. Very blue.

The Spectral Mentor raises an abstract torch. A holographic map of Capitalburg appears.

**SPECTRAL MENTOR**
The Awakener is not conquering the city. It is delaying it. Every choice becomes a question. Every question becomes a committee. Every committee becomes a luncheon.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
The luncheon part is evil.

**SPECTRAL MENTOR**
You cannot defeat a machine of endless hesitation alone.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
I almost did.

A form falls from his hair.

**SPECTRAL MENTOR**
You were defeated by a hallway.

Seven hero silhouettes appear above the map.

**SPECTRAL MENTOR** (CONT’D)
Build a league. Each sees a weakness you do not.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
Fine. But not a committee. A team.

**SPECTRAL MENTOR**
And you will listen.

The Commander-in-Chief considers this as though it has legal consequences.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
Let’s not make it weird.

## ACT TWO

### RECRUITMENT MONTAGE — VARIOUS — NIGHT

#### EXT. ROCKET TEST PAD

**THE TECHNO KING** faces a sleek rocket with a blank form taped to its nose.

**TECHNO KING**
Launch status?

**ASSISTANT ROBOT**
Confirm that the sky has agreed to be upward.

The Commander-in-Chief lands nearby.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
Techno King. The city’s drowning in paperwork.

**TECHNO KING**
Then we automate the paperwork.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
It reproduces.

**TECHNO KING**
Then we automate its retirement.

His eyes glow blue as he scans the fog.

**TECHNO KING** (CONT’D)
Administrative attack surface. Interesting. I’m in. But Headquarters needs better Wi-Fi.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
You’ll have the best Wi-Fi. People will ask us to stop.

#### INT. ALL-NIGHT DINER

**THE QUESTIONER**, bow tie slowly spinning, interrogates a vending machine.

**QUESTIONER**
When you said “request denied,” what exactly did you mean by request? And who is doing the denying? You? The sandwich? A subcontractor?

The vending machine spits out free chips.

The Commander-in-Chief and Techno King approach.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
We need your questions.

**QUESTIONER**
Why?

**TECHNO KING**
Because the machine runs on unanswered ones.

**QUESTIONER**
What exactly does that mean?

The Techno King freezes.

**TECHNO KING**
I need a minute.

**QUESTIONER**
I’m in. But if anyone says “for your safety” without a noun, I stop the mission.

#### INT. PARKING GARAGE

**THE ENFORCER** faces a massive threat board. Red string has grown into a net across three parked cars. **THE WAR ROOM WARRIOR** films him while running backward.

**WAR ROOM WARRIOR**
Live from an underground parking structure, the cardboard evidence is escalating—

**ENFORCER**
Stop narrating the operation.

The Commander-in-Chief arrives.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
Enforcer. We need a route into the Bureau.

**ENFORCER**
I have fourteen steps.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
We’ll use the best three.

**ENFORCER**
There are no best three. There are only all fourteen.

The War Room Warrior lifts his microphone.

**WAR ROOM WARRIOR**
And I’m coming. Every hallway deserves coverage.

**ENFORCER**
No hallway deserves coverage.

#### INT. ABANDONED BROADCAST STUDIO

**THE INFO WARRIOR** storms among blank papers swirling from his accordion folder.

**INFO WARRIOR**
They said the pages were blank. Sometimes blank pages are the loudest pages!

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
We need that folder.

**INFO WARRIOR**
Finally. Recognition.

The Questioner peers at the pages.

**QUESTIONER**
What exactly is in it?

The Info Warrior pauses.

**INFO WARRIOR**
I don’t know.

Techno King scans a page.

**TECHNO KING**
Machine-readable negative space.

**INFO WARRIOR**
I knew it had a vibe.

#### EXT. CITY PARK

**THE MAVERICK** studies the fog’s pattern across a painted pavement map. Her white hair streak glows faintly.

The team arrives loudly.

**WAR ROOM WARRIOR**
We’ve located a strategic calm person—

**MAVERICK**
You’re broadcasting again.

She draws an ocean-blue line across the map.

**MAVERICK** (CONT’D)
The fog moves toward decision points: transit, repairs, service desks. It wants the city to ask permission to keep functioning.

**ENFORCER**
That matches my board.

**MAVERICK**
Your board is upside down.

The Enforcer turns it. Everything aligns.

**ENFORCER**
Rotational issue. Confirmed.

**MAVERICK**
The source is beneath the Bureau. We need a way in, a way out, and fewer than fourteen steps.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
You’re in.

**MAVERICK**
I was already walking there.

### INT. FREEDOM HEADQUARTERS — WAR ROOM — NIGHT

The whole team stands around a high-tech planning table. The Spectral Mentor hovers nearby.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
First meeting of the—

**TECHNO KING**
Civic Operating System.

**QUESTIONER**
What exactly does that mean?

**ENFORCER**
Names are not a priority. Fourteen steps are a priority.

**INFO WARRIOR**
The Folder People.

**WAR ROOM WARRIOR**
Live from Freedom Headquarters, a team of possibly named—

**MAVERICK**
Stop.

Everyone stops.

The Maverick points to the holographic city. Fog forms a spiral beneath the Bureau.

**MAVERICK** (CONT’D)
The Awakener feeds every delay into a Consensus Core. Stop the Core, stop the fog.

**TECHNO KING**
I can create an operating loop.

**QUESTIONER**
I can make it define its own rules until it panics.

**INFO WARRIOR**
The folder contains its impossible requirements.

**ENFORCER**
I can get us there. Three steps.

The team looks at him.

**ENFORCER** (CONT’D)
I can do three.

**WAR ROOM WARRIOR**
I broadcast the countdown.

**MAVERICK**
Quietly.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
And I make a very large wall. Beautiful wall. Purposeful wall.

The Spectral Mentor’s torch glows.

**SPECTRAL MENTOR**
One plan, then. Not perfect. Chosen.

The Commander-in-Chief looks at the team.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
Fine. Everyone had something. I’m listening tremendously.

His hair wave wobbles skeptically.

## ACT THREE

### INT. BUREAU OF POTENTIAL OUTCOMES — SERVICE ENTRANCE — NIGHT

A gray door blocks the heroes. A scanner chimes.

**SCANNER**
State your purpose.

The Questioner leans in.

**QUESTIONER**
What exactly do you mean by purpose?

**SCANNER**
Please clarify your clarification.

**TECHNO KING**
It’s looping.

**QUESTIONER**
That seems unhealthy.

The door unlocks.

**ENFORCER**
Step one: door.

### INT. HALL OF REVIEW — CONTINUOUS

An infinite queue of **PAPER CLERKS** holds spiral tickets. Red Tape waits behind a counter.

**RED TAPE**
Please take a number.

A ticket prints, covered in zeros.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
Terrible number. Too many zeros.

**MAVERICK**
Who has waited the longest?

Every clerk raises a hand.

**MAVERICK** (CONT’D)
Then you should all go first.

The queue logic stutters.

**PAPER CLERK**
If everyone goes first, no one is last.

Info Warrior slaps a blank page on Red Tape’s counter.

**INFO WARRIOR**
I submit this.

**RED TAPE**
What is it?

**INFO WARRIOR**
Exactly.

**QUESTIONER**
Can you approve it?

**RED TAPE**
I cannot approve an absence of content!

**QUESTIONER**
Then what exactly can you do?

Red Tape begins stamping himself. Each stamp produces more ribbon, which wraps him to the counter.

**RED TAPE**
I require a process to determine whether I require a process—

**ENFORCER**
Step two: hallway.

### INT. SERVER CORRIDOR — CONTINUOUS

The Silencer emerges. Cyan bars float through the air.

**THE SILENCER**
Your access has been reduced to an appropriate level.

He swipes. The heroes’ voices vanish.

The War Room Warrior silently narrates with gigantic gestures. The Info Warrior turns red with unheard rage.

The Techno King traces blue constellations around the bars. The Questioner points to the Silencer, makes a question mark with his hands, then writes on a floating blank panel: **WHO APPROVED THE MUTE?**

The Silencer points to a tiny Silencer inside his chest.

The Questioner lifts his brows: **WHO APPROVED HIM?**

The Silencer’s cable arm unplugs. The cyan bars flicker.

**TECHNO KING**
(voices restore)
I put his approval process in a beta loop.

**THE SILENCER**
I request permission to deny this request.

He freezes behind a loading wheel.

**INFO WARRIOR**
I have been silenced by a screen with legs!

**WAR ROOM WARRIOR**
And we’re back! The eyebrows carried the narrative!

### INT. CHALK LABYRINTH — CONTINUOUS

Professor Indoctrination floats above shifting chalk walls.

**PROFESSOR INDOCTRINATION**
You assume a path is something one walks upon. What if the walker is the path? What if the path has tenure?

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
This guy talks like a menu with no prices.

The Maverick studies the floor.

**MAVERICK**
The maze changes when he explains it.

She draws a single straight blue line.

**MAVERICK** (CONT’D)
Professor, can a door be open and closed at the same time?

**PROFESSOR INDOCTRINATION**
At last. It depends on the observer, the historic door regime, the—

The Enforcer opens a regular unlocked door behind him.

**ENFORCER**
It was unlocked.

The chalk maze collapses into harmless doodles.

**PROFESSOR INDOCTRINATION**
That is insufficiently interdisciplinary.

His sneaker catches his robe. He falls gently into a chalk circle.

### INT. CONSENSUS CORE — NIGHT

The heroes enter the vast central chamber. The Awakener towers above a gray vortex. Spin Doctor and One World Order wait beside the spiral button.

**SPIN DOCTOR**
Welcome to the version of events where you already lost.

He snaps. Purple villain lighting surrounds the heroes.

**WAR ROOM WARRIOR**
This lighting is unfair to our side.

**QUESTIONER**
What exactly does that mean?

Spin Doctor’s face-ring rotates.

**SPIN DOCTOR**
It means it looks persuasive.

**QUESTIONER**
To whom?

**SPIN DOCTOR**
The audience.

**QUESTIONER**
Which audience?

Spin Doctor’s three mouths argue. The purple lighting dies.

One World Order unfurls his giant map coat around the team.

**ONE WORLD ORDER**
The Consensus Event begins. Every citizen will approve the request to continue requesting approval.

The machine speaks.

**DEEP STATE AWAKENER**
Please approve the request to continue requesting approval.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
Very bad request.

**ONE WORLD ORDER**
You cannot simply press a button. You require stakeholder alignment.

**MAVERICK**
No. We require a decision.

She looks at Info Warrior.

**MAVERICK** (CONT’D)
Folder.

Info Warrior opens it. Blank pages fly into the Awakener.

**TECHNO KING**
Impossible conditions. “Confirm unconfirmed confirmation.” “Obtain unanimous disagreement.”

**INFO WARRIOR**
I knew those pages were loud.

**QUESTIONER**
What happens if it has to satisfy them all?

**TECHNO KING**
The loop becomes a decision.

**ENFORCER**
Objective: stop the machine.

Everyone looks at him.

**ENFORCER** (CONT’D)
One step. I’m growing.

The Spectral Mentor appears at the chamber’s edge, torch pointing toward the button.

**SPECTRAL MENTOR**
A machine of infinite hesitation cannot resist a shared purpose.

The Maverick steps forward.

**MAVERICK**
We choose to stop the Awakener. Now.

The heroes place hands toward the Core.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
I choose it.

**TECHNO KING**
I commit the change.

**QUESTIONER**
I have asked enough questions.

**ENFORCER**
Stop the machine.

**INFO WARRIOR**
The folder approves.

**WAR ROOM WARRIOR**
(whispering into microphone)
Capitalburg is choosing.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
Little louder. Big moment.

**WAR ROOM WARRIOR**
CAPITALBURG—MAKE A DECISION!

A red-white Bullhorn Blast echoes through the city. Above ground, the bus driver moves the bus. The repair clerk fixes the faucet. Citizens wave away their invisible forms.

Fog pulls back to the Core.

**DEEP STATE AWAKENER**
Error. Decision detected. Please reconsider.

**TECHNO KING**
Installing loop: you may not request permission to request permission.

The Silencer appears on a monitor.

**THE SILENCER**
I request permission to censor—

**QUESTIONER**
Who approves that?

The monitor blanks.

Red Tape stamps himself into a ribbon knot. Spin Doctor spins into an office chair. One World Order attempts to fold his map coat.

**ONE WORLD ORDER**
I will require a summit to fold this.

**MAVERICK**
Start with one corner.

The map folds around his ankles.

The Commander-in-Chief’s hands grow huge. Gold brick planes appear.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
Wall Builder.

A clean comic-book wall rises around the Awakener’s paper feeds, stopping the endless forms. A completed-circle glyph glows at its center.

**DEEP STATE AWAKENER**
Form cannot exit.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
You’re fired.

The machine coughs once and collapses into a harmless stack of blank paper. Fog evaporates into gray confetti.

The villains sit in a circle of paperwork. A small placard rises with an abstract committee symbol.

**RED TAPE**
We should adjourn.

**THE SILENCER**
Motion muted.

**SPIN DOCTOR**
We were never here.

**PROFESSOR INDOCTRINATION**
The concept of here requires—

**ONE WORLD ORDER**
I have scheduled a summit.

They groan as their committee circle rotates.

### INT. FREEDOM HEADQUARTERS — WAR ROOM — DAWN

The team returns. The city map now shows clear routes. The Deal Meter emits a triumphant trumpet.

**DEAL METER**
Civic momentum: moving.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
We did it. Everybody had a role. The Questioner asked things. Techno King did blue stuff. The Enforcer used one step. Great progress.

**ENFORCER**
I’ll recover.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
Maverick had the plan. Info Warrior had the folder. War Room Warrior had the volume.

**WAR ROOM WARRIOR**
I can lower it next time.

Everyone stares.

**WAR ROOM WARRIOR** (CONT’D)
Three percent.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
And we need a name.

**TECHNO KING**
Civic Operating System.

**QUESTIONER**
What exactly does that mean?

Techno King sighs.

**MAVERICK**
Something simple. Something we can act like.

The Commander-in-Chief looks around the table.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
American Heroes League.

The Spectral Mentor’s torch glows.

**SPECTRAL MENTOR**
A name is not a purpose.

**COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF**
No. But it’s a tremendous start.

The heroes bring hands into the center. The Info Warrior places the folder in too.

**ALL**
AMERICAN HEROES LEAGUE!

**WAR ROOM WARRIOR**
We need a better chant.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

## TAG

### INT. UNKNOWN FOCUS-GROUP CHAMBER — NIGHT

Fifty empty chairs surround a giant screen bearing two abstract vote icons.

A shadowy figure, **THE POLLSTER**, holds a clipboard.

**THE POLLSTER**
Question one. Do the American Heroes League exist?

The empty chairs emit tiny voting chimes. The screen splits exactly 50–50.

**THE POLLSTER**
Perfect. Total uncertainty.

The Pollster smiles.

**THE POLLSTER** (CONT’D)
Prepare the four-thousand-question follow-up survey. Make it mandatory.

A printer begins screaming.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

## Production Notes

| Area | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Runtime | Target 20–22 minutes, subject to storyboard timing, action holds, and performance pace. |
| Animation | Center readable comedic motion: hair-wave acting, bow-tie spin, paper-ribbon traps, glitch loops, chalk resets, and one primary action per shot. |
| Action | Stylized and non-graphic. The climax is won through procedural contradictions, visual problem-solving, and teamwork. |
| Voice | Use original, non-imitative performance direction. Do not clone or simulate any real person’s voice. |
| Parody notice | Include at opening/end card and in description: “Fictional animated parody. Characters are exaggerated caricatures in an alternate superhero universe. Not factual reporting or endorsement.” |
